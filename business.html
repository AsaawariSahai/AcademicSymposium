Segment Overview

The Business Product Pitch Segment invites students of Classes 11 and 12 to develop a single business idea into a fully reasoned, evidence-backed venture proposal revolving around the Circular and Gig Economy. Students may either build on an idea they have previously presented at a school-level competition, strengthening it with deeper research, data and strategy, or develop a completely original idea on the themes given.

  • Each school can only enroll one team.
  • Each team must consist of 4 students.
  • Only one of the topics has to be chosen for the presentation.
  • The final product should be an enhanced version of the initial prototype (not a new or different product)
  • Please note that once the Expression of Interest Form is filled up, a separate Registration Form will be sent out to the schools. The details of the participants must be filled in the Registration Form. No changes will be accepted after the form has been submitted.

Themes & Scope

Each team must develop its product, service or business model around one of the following themes.

Circular Economy

A business model that minimises waste by keeping products and materials in use through reuse, repair, refurbishment and recycling, rather than following the traditional “take–make–dispose” approach.

Gig Economy

A labour model based on short-term, flexible or on-demand work rather than permanent employment, generally coordinated through digital platforms.

Selection Process

  • Stage 1 : Selection & Notification – First-come, first-served: Once the Expression of Interest form closes on Friday, 25th September, 2026, the first 12 schools to fill up the form will be invited to participate.
    – Status Notification: Confirmed schools are notified via email on Saturday, 26th September, 2026.
  • Stage 2 : Initial Submission – Submission Requirement : The 12 registered schools must submit their initial product layout as a presentation.
    – Submission Method : Presentations must be sent via email using the link provided on the official website.
  • Stage 3 : Evaluation & Elimination – Assessment Criteria : Submissions will be evaluated on credibility, viability, social responsibility, relevance, and scalability.
    – Shortlisting Process : The top 6 schools will advance to the next stage to deliver a detailed presentation at the Academic Symposium.

Research, Submission & Presentation Requirements

Registered schools are required to submit a brief layout of their product as a presentation.

  • Format - PPTX or PDF.
  • Length - Strictly 10 to 12 slides.
  • Enhancements - Relevant photos, videos and external add-ons are welcome but must fit within the slide limit.

Brief structural template:

  1. Comprehensive problem statement analysis
  2. The proposed product prototype or layout
  3. A solid proof of concept
  4. Brief financial projections
