Full-Day Schedule

The symposium runs in two parts on a single day at Modern High School for Girls.

Segment 1

Research, Technology & Society

The top six selected essays in each track of the research paper segment are presented by their respective authors.

8:30 a.m. – 9:00 a.m. Registration of schools for - Research, Technology & Society
9:00 a.m. – 9:30 a.m. Welcome address by MHS
Keynote address by Dr Manoshi Roychowdhury, Co-Chairperson, Techno India Group
9:30 a.m. – 10:30 a.m. Technical Research: Cybersecurity and Digital Infrastructure — 5 minutes presentation + 5 minutes audience Q&A per paper
10:30 a.m. – 10:45 a.m. Short break
10:45 a.m. – 11:00 a.m. Welcome address by MHS
Keynote address by Dr Saikat Majumdar, Senior Scientist, CSIR – Indian Institute of Chemical Biology
11:00 a.m. – 12:00 noon Interdisciplinary Research: Technology and Society — 5 minutes presentation + 5 minutes audience Q&A per paper
12:00 nooon– 1:00 p.m. Lunch break
Segment 2

From Idea to Enterprise: Business Product Pitch

The top six shortlisted teams participate in this segment.

12:30 p.m. – 1:00 p.m. Registration of schools for - From Idea to Enterprise: Business Product Pitch
1:00 p.m. – 1:15 p.m. Welcome address by MHS
Keynote address by Ms Swati Gautam, Kolkata-based entrepreneur, and Managing Trustee of the NGO Pukaar
1:15 p.m.– 3:15 p.m. From Idea to Enterprise: Business Product Pitch — 15 minutes allotted per school, followed by questions from judges
3:15 p.m. – 3:30 p.m. Closing Ceremony
