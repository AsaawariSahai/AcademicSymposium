Key Dates

5th October, 2026
Business Pitch Submission — Segment 2
All 12 registered schools submit a brief but comprehensive presentation of their product, optionally accompanied by video or pictures of a prototype.
9th October, 2026
Research Paper Submission — Segment 1
All interested participants submit their IT Technical or Interdisciplinary papers to the host school.
15th October, 2026
Business Shortlist Announced — Segment 2
Submitted presentations undergo preliminary screening. The host school notifies the six qualifying schools of their selection.
24th October, 2026
IT Shortlist Announced — Segment 1
Twelve papers — six technical, six interdisciplinary — are selected and the qualifying participants are notified.
5th November, 2026
Academic Symposium
Shortlisted schools present their final papers and pitches at Modern High School for Girls, Kolkata.

How Registration Works

  • The invitation is extended to twenty schools across Kolkata.
  • For the Business Product Pitch, registration is first come, first served — only the first twelve schools to register may participate.
  • For the IT Research Paper track, all interested participants from registered schools may submit papers by the deadline above.
  • Submitted work undergoes preliminary screening before shortlists are announced.
  • Shortlisted schools present their final work, live, on the day of the symposium.

Queries
