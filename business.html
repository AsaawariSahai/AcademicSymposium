Business Pitch

From Idea to Enterprise — a research-backed pitch by student founders. Teams of four develop a single business idea into a fully reasoned, evidence-backed venture proposal.

Teams of 4 7-min pitch + 5-min panel Q&A Grades 11 & 12 6 Finalist Teams

Introduction & Rationale

The Business Symposium is an academic event for students of Classes 11–12, designed to go beyond conventional project displays and shark-tank-style competitions. It invites students to develop a single business idea into a fully reasoned, evidence-backed venture proposal — either building on an idea previously presented at a school-level competition and strengthening it with deeper research, data and strategy, or starting fresh.

Objectives

  • Cultivate entrepreneurial thinking, financial literacy, and evidence-based reasoning among students.
  • Teach students to turn a raw idea into a structured business case using real analytical frameworks — SWOT, USP, scalability, and CSR.
  • Reward depth of research and iteration by allowing improvement of previously presented ideas, rather than demanding novelty alone.
  • Connect students with a panel of field experts to determine the viability and credibility of the product innovation.
  • Embed social responsibility and sustainability thinking into student enterprise from an early stage.

Team & Categories

Team Composition

Each team comprises four students, who together present different aspects of the product — its financing, usability, market value, and research.

Elevated Idea

A concept previously presented at a school-level event (exhibition, fest, or competition), now revisited with fresh research, financial modelling, and a viability study.

New Venture

An entirely original idea developed from scratch for the symposium. Both categories are judged equally — there is no separate scoring track for either.

Themes for the Product

Circular Economy

A business model that minimises waste by keeping products and materials in use through reuse, repair, refurbishment, and recycling, instead of the traditional take-make-dispose approach.

Gig Economy

A labour model based on short-term, flexible, or on-demand work rather than permanent employment, coordinated mainly through digital platforms.

Important Notes

  • The prototype must be safe and functional.
  • Working models are strongly encouraged.
  • Tech solutions: show working code or a physical prototype.
  • Non-tech solutions: mockups, storyboards, or visual walkthroughs are acceptable.

Stages of Competition

Each team presents for seven minutes — a limit that must not be exceeded — showcasing the product in its final form rather than a prototype. This is followed by five minutes of panel cross-questioning, after which the floor opens to audience questions for deeper discussion. Teams must conduct primary research (a short survey, 10–15 interviews, or a field test) and secondary research (industry reports, government data, news) to support the product’s viability.

Mandatory Elements of Every Pitch

•
Problem StatementThe specific problem being solved, with evidence that it exists.
•
SWOT AnalysisAn honest evaluation of Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.
•
Unique Selling PropositionWhat sets the idea apart from existing alternatives.
•
Target Market & SegmentWho the product/service is for, with basic market sizing (TAM-SAM-SOM, if possible).
•
Business & Revenue ModelHow the venture earns money — pricing, channels, and cost structure.
•
Scalability PlanHow the idea can grow beyond its pilot stage — geographically, digitally, or across product lines.
•
Viability & Financial ReasoningApproximate cost estimates, break-even reasoning, and funding requirements, even in simplified form.
•
Competitive AnalysisAt least two comparable products/services and how the idea outperforms them.
•
CSR / Sustainability AngleThe social, environmental, or community benefit built into the venture from the start, not added afterward.
•
Risk Analysis & MitigationKey risks to the idea and how the team plans to manage them.
•
Prototype / Proof of ConceptA model, sample, app mock-up, working demo, or detailed visual mock-up that makes the idea tangible.
